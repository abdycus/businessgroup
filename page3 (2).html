01 / Our fictional origin story

Big ambitions.
Local roots.

In this fictional classroom story, our version of Wells Fargo began when three former college classmates reunited at a campus entrepreneurship fair. Zach, a lead financial accountant, Matt, a financial analyst, and Abby, a Cyber Security Application Penetration Testing Engineer, shared a vision: a banking firm that combined clear financial information, thoughtful analysis, and carefully tested digital services.

In our fictional story, the founders started in a borrowed campus office with three desks and a whiteboard. Zach established accounting procedures and financial reports, Matt researched customer needs and built financial forecasts, and Abby tested the prototype banking application for weaknesses. Together, they turned a classroom-sized idea into an imagined community banking and financial services firm.

Today, our imagined firm brings together everyday banking, savings services, lending, and financial planning. Our mission is to make every conversation welcoming and every next step easier to understand.

The people behind the purpose.

Zach oversees the firm's fictional financial records, prepares financial statements, and coordinates account reconciliations. His attention to detail helps the team understand its results, and he enjoys making complex accounting information easier to follow.

04 / Frequently asked questions

A little clarity goes a long way.

What services does this fictional firm offer?

Our fictional service lineup includes checking and savings accounts, personal and small-business loans, digital banking, and financial-planning consultations.

Can I open a real account on this website?

No. This site is a class project showcasing an imagined banking firm. It does not open accounts or accept deposits.

Do I need a giant piggy bank to visit?

Definitely not! In our fictional branch, every goal is welcome, whether you're imagining your first savings account or planning your dream business.

Can my student club request a money workshop?

Yes! As part of our fictional community program, clubs can request a Money Basics session covering budgeting vocabulary and financial goal-setting.

Can I bring my lucky calculator?

Of course. Our imaginary team appreciates a good calculator, especially one with a history of surviving accounting exams.
